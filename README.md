## ⚙️ Quick Setup Guide (Takes 1 Minute)

### 1. Enable Wireless Debugging on your TV (One-Time Setup)
* **Google TV / Chromecast / Onn 4K / Sony / TCL**:
  1. Go to **Settings → System (or About) → Android TV OS Build**.
  2. Click **Build 7 times** until you see *"You are now a developer!"*.
  3. Go back to **System → Developer Options**.
  4. Enable **Wireless Debugging** (and **USB Debugging**).
* **Nvidia Shield TV**:
  1. Go to **Settings → Device Preferences → About → Build** (click 7 times).
  2. Go back to **Device Preferences → Developer Options**.
  3. Turn on **Network Debugging**.

### 2. Connect ATV Optimizer on your Phone
1. Install and open **ATV Optimizer** on your Android phone or tablet.
2. Tap **Scan Network** (ensure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi).
3. Select your TV from the list.
4. On your TV screen, check **"Always allow from this network"** and click **Allow**.
